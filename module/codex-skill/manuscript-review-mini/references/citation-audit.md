# Citation Audit

Check citation existence and DOI/authors/title/year/journal/publication status with
nature-ref-verifier as needed. Agent checks whether each statement is supported, citation
placement, overconcentration, competing views, primary versus secondary evidence and
citation laundering. Reopen high-risk numbers/methods/claims via nature-reader. Invented
citations never pass. Compare section-level coverage with the actual argument: foundations,
direct competitors, methods, comparisons and contrary evidence where relevant. Targeted
academic-search may fill a documented coverage gap. New direct competitor requires re-evaluation of claim,
novelty, journal fit and required experiments, not a cosmetic citation. No broad Stage 1 restart.

Inherit any citation baseline, counting scope and fixed target from the writing plan;
never double the current count again or impose a new target on legacy drafts without one.
In the existing combined audit's citation section, record actual unique cited works in
main text (including captions), SI and their union; resolve duplicate keys/versions before
comparison. Check unexplained shortfalls and additions that supply no relevant support.
Do not remove useful independent evidence merely to shorten prose, or preserve irrelevant
citations to satisfy a number. Explain substantive additions/removals in existing revision
records and carry any unresolved target decision forward; do not silently approve it.
Use normal revision routing and risk assessment according to scientific impact, not the
number of added references. Citation counts do not substitute for support verification.
