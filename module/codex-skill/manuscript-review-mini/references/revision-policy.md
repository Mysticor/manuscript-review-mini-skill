# Revision Policy

Plan blocker→major→moderate→minor with concern, decision/action, sections/displays/
claims, required source and expected outcome before edits. Log each action, source,
version and closure result. Preserve paragraphs without identified problems. Integrate
local fixes naturally, without repetitive defensive additions, revision scars, extra
emphasis or unnecessary word substitutions. Use revision/changes.md for planned actions and outcomes. Maintain versions at actual Round1 revision,
polishing or Round2 revision when performed, and final; no version for every typo. A correction should
solve its issue without widening claims or silently changing the scientific narrative.
